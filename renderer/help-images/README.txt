Drop your own screenshots in here to replace the diagrams in the Help tab.

The Help tab uses inline SVG diagrams for layout concepts, but you can replace
or supplement them with real screenshots by adding PNG/JPG files here and
inserting <img src="help-images/your-file.png"/> tags into the relevant
section of renderer/index.html (search for "Help tab" or the section heading).

Suggested filenames the Help tab can reference:
  window-overview.png       - whole app showing header + rail + webview
  rail-buttons.png          - close-up of the four rail buttons labeled
  voice-mode-flow.png       - voice transcription pasting into a chat
  caption-area-select.png   - the drag-to-select overlay in action
  caption-mode-flow.png     - OCR'd caption appearing in chat input
  hotkeys-tab.png           - the Hotkeys settings tab
  stealth-toggle.png        - red vs green stealth dot states
