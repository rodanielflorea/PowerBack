What this app does

RemoteDevJobsAce listens to your interview call, turns what the interviewer says into text, and writes a spoken-style answer for you in seconds — based on your CV, the job description and any notes you upload. You read the answer and say it in your own words. It stays hidden from screen sharing and recordings.

First time: activation

  1. On the first start you see Activate this computer. Click Copy next to the computer ID and send it to your administrator.
  2. Paste the license key you get back and click Activate.
  3. That's it — you will not be asked again until the license expires.

Starting an interview (3 steps)

On the welcome screen click Interview mode.

  1. Interview stage — pick what kind of call it is. Intro = recruiter screening (simple, friendly words). Technical = precise, factual answers about what you built. HR = ownership, teamwork, expectations. CEO = product thinking and business impact. This changes how every answer is written, so choose carefully.
  2. Your profile — your name, country, city and timezone. Pick a city and the timezone fills in by itself. Click ＋ to save the profile so next time you just pick it from the list.
  3. Materials — drag in your CV and the job description (PDF, Word or text). Add any extra notes as Support material, and earlier interview notes as Previous meeting records. Optionally enter your salary expectation (amount, currency, per month / year / hour) so the app can answer that question the way you want.

Click Start interview. Listening starts automatically.

During the interview

  • Blue bubbles are what the interviewer said. When a question ends, the answer appears underneath it.
  • On each bubble: ↻ answers it again, ✎ lets you fix the transcript and then answer, × removes it.
  • Type your own question in the box at the bottom and press Enter (or the arrow) — for example when you want to prepare an answer before they ask.
  • Text / Code / Diagram choose the answer format. Use Code for coding questions and Diagram for architecture questions.
  • The drop-down at the top switches the interview stage mid-call if the tone changes.
  • Clear removes all bubbles and answers (your materials stay).

The round "A" button

A small round button floats on your screen from the moment the app starts. Click it to hide the app, click again to bring it back — handy if you hid the window and forgot the hotkey. It is hidden from screen sharing too, and you can drag it by its edge to any corner.

Staying invisible

  • Keep Stealth on (crossed-out green eye). Then the app does not appear in Zoom, Meet or Teams screen sharing, nor in recordings.
  • Stealth works on Windows. On macOS it works for most apps, but recent Zoom/Teams versions can still capture the window. On Linux no app can hide itself from screen capture — hide the window with Ctrl+Alt+H while you share your screen.
  • Click-through works on Windows, macOS and Linux X11 sessions; Ubuntu's default Wayland session does not support it (the button is greyed out).
  • Make the window see-through with Ctrl+Alt+] (more visible) and Ctrl+Alt+[ (more transparent).
  • Hide and show instantly with Ctrl+Alt+H.
  • Move the window with Ctrl+Alt+Arrow keys.

The info panel

When an interview starts, a small panel opens with the local time for your profile city, the weather, upcoming holidays and — if you uploaded a CV — your work history (company, place, period, role, remote / hybrid / onsite) and education. Use it as a quick reminder when they ask "walk me through your CV".

Write to IDE (typing the code for you)

When an answer contains code, click Write to IDE on that code block, then click into your editor. The app types the code there like a person would — with natural pauses and the occasional corrected typo. Move your mouse to pause; it continues when you stop. The 1–5 slider sets the speed.

Tips for better answers

  • Always upload your CV and the job description — answers are only as good as what the app knows about you.
  • Pick the right stage. A technical answer in a recruiter call sounds odd, and vice versa.
  • If a transcript came out wrong, click ✎, fix the words, and answer again.
  • Read the answer and say it naturally; you do not have to use every word.

If something goes wrong

No transcript appears

Check the microphone button is red (listening). Open Settings → Providers and make sure Microphone and/or System audio are ticked — system audio is what captures the other person in Zoom, Meet or Teams. Then stop and start listening again.

"API key was rejected" or "rate limit"

The app's built-in key is not working. Contact your administrator.

The window disappeared

Press Ctrl+Alt+H or click the round A button. If it is see-through, press Ctrl+Alt+] a few times.

Clicks do nothing on the app

Click-through is on (red cursor icon). Press Alt+Q.

"License expired"

Send your computer ID to your administrator again to get a new key.
