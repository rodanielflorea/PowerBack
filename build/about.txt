About Ace

Ace is a real-time meeting and interview assistant. It listens to a call,
transcribes what is said, and suggests spoken answers, code and diagrams on a
small always-on-top window that stays hidden from screen sharing.

What it does
  - Live transcription of the meeting, with the speaker's words on screen.
  - AI answers on demand (never automatically): press Send or Ctrl+Enter.
  - Code and diagram answers, each openable in its own note.
  - A supporter can join to see your screen, chat, and — with your screen
    shared — control it to help hands-on.
  - Stealth: the window is hidden from screen-capture and screen-sharing.

This installer
  - Installs Ace for the current user (no administrator rights needed).
  - Lets you choose the install folder on the next screen.
  - Adds a Desktop and Start Menu shortcut.
  - Removes any previous version of Ace automatically.

Using Ace responsibly
  Ace is a preparation and accessibility aid. Make sure your use of it follows
  the rules of the meeting, interview or exam you take part in, and the laws on
  recording and consent where you and the other participants are. You are
  responsible for how you use it.
